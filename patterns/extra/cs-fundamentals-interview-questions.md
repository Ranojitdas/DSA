# 📘 CS Fundamentals Interview Preparation — Question Checklist

**Focus:** OOP, DBMS, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, SQL, C++/programming fundamentals, backend basics, and project discussion.

This checklist combines core CS questions with topics mentioned in publicly available candidate interview reports for L&T/LTIMindtree and KPMG-related fresher interviews. Interview reports are anecdotal and vary by role, campus, and year; this is a preparation checklist, not a guaranteed question set.
